### 🎾 What I build

| | |
|---|---|
| 📅 **Booking systems** | Court reservations, open matches, coaching sessions, conflict checks, payments |
| 🪪 **Membership systems** | Plans, passes, credits, renewals and multi-club member accounts |
| 📊 **Scoreboards** | Live padel scoring on courtside screens, tablets and phones |
| 🏆 **Tournament managers** | Draws, groups, brackets, scheduling and live results |

### 🛠️ How I build

- **Backend:** Go and Ruby on Rails: multi-tenant APIs, PostgreSQL, spec-first OpenAPI
- **Mobile:** Swift (iOS) and Kotlin (Android) apps for players and club staff
- **Web:** Next.js dashboards and booking flows
- **Product:** from the first conversation with a club owner to the app in their players' hands

### 📍 Where

Based in Shanghai 🇨🇳, building for clubs across Asia and beyond.
